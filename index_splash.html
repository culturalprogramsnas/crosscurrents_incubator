Crosscurrents Incubator Roster

The Crosscurrents Incubator will take place November 10 - 13, 2026, at Atelier de la Nature, Arnaudville, Louisiana, near Lafayette. This site features a roster of the 30 participants and their initial assigned challenge / working group, as well the hosts, staff, advisors, and special guests who will be at the convening. It can be sorted by name, filtered by working group or role, and searched by name, working group, institution, or role.

Information provided here is solely for facilitating collaboration between the Crosscurrents Incubator attendees. Please do not share it outside of our community.
